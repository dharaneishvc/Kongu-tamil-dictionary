# images/

Pictures for dictionary entries. Referenced from the `image` column of
`data/entries.csv` **by filename only**:

```csv
123,ஒல்லு,,ollu,,நெல் குத்தும் செக்கு,husking mill,ஒல்லுல நெல்லக் குத்திட்டு வா,,farming_livestock,,,ollu.jpg
```

Guidelines:

* Lowercase filename, no spaces — `ollu.jpg`, `kotaappu.webp`.
* JPG or WebP, at least 800 px wide, under ~300 KB.
* The picture must be your own, or licensed for commercial reuse.
  Record the origin in the entry's `notes` column when it is not yours.
* Landscape crops look best; cards render them at a 16:9-ish ratio.

The site shows the image as a card thumbnail and full width in the detail panel.
No code change is needed after adding one.
